# Sources for OPEC+ decisions added in November 2026

The list in `opec_decisions.csv` records **major** OPEC+ decisions only: cuts,
increases, extensions of restraint, and failed or no-cut meetings. Routine
monthly meetings that just reaffirm an earlier decision are left out.

Categories used for the Nov 2025 – Sep 2026 entries:
- **increase:** a rise in output targets for the following month.
- **extend:** keeping restraint in place or halting increases (Nov 30, 2025: 2026
  levels and the first-quarter pause kept; Sep 6, 2026: increases halted after
  the 2023 cuts were fully unwound).
- Left out as routine reaffirmations: Jan 4 and Feb 1, 2026 (pause reaffirmed),
  Oct 4 and Nov 1, 2026 (targets held steady after the Sep 6 halt).
